How the numbers work

  • "Mapping" means choosing the value for one field on an invoice by hand.
  • Both tools can suggest the GL account from a vendor default, so it counts the same on both sides and is left out of the saving.
  • A typical tool fills 1 field (GL account) or 2 (GL account, plus entity when each entity has its own inbox). The rest are mapped by hand.
  • Ottimate sets entity, location and department from where the invoice lands: 3 of 4 fields, or 75%. With 3 fields it is 2 of 3, or 67%.
  • Hours = invoices × fields the typical tool leaves to your team × seconds per field. One coding pass per invoice. Invoices with many separately coded lines save more.
  • "Up to" because invoices split across several locations, or needing a manual override, save less.
