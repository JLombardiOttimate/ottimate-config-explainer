How the numbers work

  • "Coding" means choosing one part of the GL string on an invoice. The saving is the share of those parts that Ottimate fills in from the invoice's location.
  • 3-part example: the store and department fill in, and Natural GL is left to confirm. That is 2 of 3 parts, or 67%.
  • 4-part example: entity, store and department fill in, and Natural GL is left to confirm. That is 3 of 4 parts, or 75%.
  • Natural GL often fills in from the vendor's default GL. The counts above still treat it as work, so the saving is understated for those vendors.
  • Hours = invoices × parts Ottimate fills in × seconds per part. The example counts one coding pass per invoice, and invoices with many separately coded lines save more.
  • "Up to" because invoices split across several stores, or needing a manual override, save less.
